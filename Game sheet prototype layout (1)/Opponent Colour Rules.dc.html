OPPONENT COLOUR — CLASH CONTINGENCIES

BGM is red on every sheet, in every mode, regardless of jersey worn. That fixed point creates two failure cases — opponents whose brand colour is red, and opponents whose brand colour is black. This spec resolves both.

01 THE FIXED POINT
RESERVED
BGM RED
#E84131 · OKLCH 62 / .20 / 29°

Red means us. It is never issued to an opponent, never softened, never swapped for the white or black jersey. An opponent may share the colour in real life; they do not share it on the sheet.

ISSUED
OPPONENT
RESOLVED, NOT RAW

The opponent's brand hex is an input, not the output. Every opponent colour passes the resolver before it reaches a score, a bar, or a crest ring.

All four are cool-side. Nothing in the set can be mistaken for BGM red, for WIN emerald, or for OTL amber. Assignment is deterministic on the club abbreviation, so a given opponent always draws the same alternate — and two red clubs in one view never land on the same colour.

Black clubs do not draw from the set. A neutral has no hue worth preserving, so it lifts straight up the neutral axis to gunmetal — honest to the kit, and never a colour the club never wore.

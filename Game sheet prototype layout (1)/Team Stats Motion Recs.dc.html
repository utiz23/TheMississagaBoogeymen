Recommended motion Eight cues — the stat sheet, dueling out from the middle The head-to-head reads like a broadcast comparison graphic. Rows deal in top to bottom; each stat's two share bars grow outward from the center divider as the numbers tick up, and only the winning side earns a single flare. Losers stay dim. Nothing loops at rest.
1
Panel reveal + hairline wipe
Rise 20px + fade, .55s ease-out; a red 2px hairline wipes across the top edge once as the panel plugs in. t=0
2
Rows deal in top → bottom
Each stat row (label + both values + bar) rises and fades in sequence, ~105ms apart — the sheet fills like a comparison card being read out. t=.30s
3
Bars grow outward from center
As each row lands, its two share bars grow from the 2px center divider toward their edges — BGM leftward, opponent rightward. A tug-of-war, not two separate fills. .5s / row
4
Integer values count up
Counting stats (goals, shots, hits) tick 0 → value in sync with the bar; rate and clock stats (On Net %, Possession) simply fade in — no fake counting. with bar
5
Winning side flares once
The side that won each stat gets a single settle-flare as it lands — red glow for BGM, a cool white glow for the opponent. The losing bar stays dim. One event, never a pulse. on land
6
Hover a row — read affordance
Hovering a stat steps its background to surface-raised and brightens the label, so a reader can isolate one comparison. Border-only, no lift. hover
7
CTA arrow nudges
"FULL HEAD-TO-HEAD →" slides its arrow 3px right on hover — the only motion invited after the sheet settles. hover
8
At rest, it holds still
No ambient loop, no shimmer, no ticker. Once resolved, the sheet is a static reference — motion earns its place, then gets out of the way. rest
Guardrails Full entrance ≤ 1.2s · rows stagger, never all at once · bars grow from the divider outward, they don't slide in from the sides · one flare per row, on the winner only · rate / clock stats never fake-count · no idle animation once settled · all motion off under reduced-motion (bars and numbers snap to final).

Recommended motion Eight cues — the game plotting itself The map should feel like the match replotting onto the ice: the rink lays down, then each event drops onto its exact spot in chronological order. Goals flare as they land. Hover, select, and filter are broadcast-crisp — no shimmer, no bounce, one measured beat per event.
1
Rink panel rises in
The whole broadcast panel — hairline border, ice, filters — lifts and fades in as one surface. The stage, before any play. t=0 · .5s
2
Ice settles, red ticker wipe
Lines, circles and creases fade up together while a single thin red wipe sweeps left→right across the rink — a TV lower-third bringing the ice online. t≈.15s
3
Markers drop onto the ice
Each event falls from above onto its exact coordinate with a short landing squash, staggered ~95ms in strict chronological order — the game replaying itself spatially. t≈.52s+
4
Goals flare as they land
A goal marker lands with one red glow that settles — BGM goals in accent red, 4L goals in white. Stronger than a shot or hit, marked exactly once. on goal
5
Event list mirrors the map
As each goal lands on the ice, its row slides in from the right in the synced list — map and list stay in lockstep. per goal
6
Hover raises a halo + tooltip
Hovering a marker rings it with a soft halo and lifts a broadcast tooltip — actor, side, period, time — above the puck. Instant, ≤.15s. on hover
7
Select locks the pair
Clicking a marker (or its list row) locks a strong red halo on the ice and highlights the matching row at once — one settle, no pulse. The pair reads as one. on click
8
Filter fades in place
Toggling a type off dims those markers to 16% ghosts where they sit — never removed, never reflowed. Spatial memory is preserved so the eye keeps its map. on toggle
Guardrails Total plot-in ≤ ~2s and caps · markers fall ~120 units with one landing squash, never a bounce · one glow per goal, never a looping pulse (nothing on the ice breathes) · markers never change position on filter — they fade in place · hover/select resolve in ≤.15s · a dense game plots the first ~24 events on load, the rest appear instantly on scroll/filter · under reduced-motion everything is plotted, wipe and flares off.
